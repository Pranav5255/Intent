# Google Pixel 10 -- Feature List

> **Note:** This document summarizes the expected and publicly announced
> feature set of the Pixel 10 family. Exact availability may vary by
> model and region.

## Display

-   OLED display with high refresh rate (up to 120 Hz on supported
    models)
-   HDR support
-   Always-On Display
-   Adaptive brightness
-   Gorilla Glass protection (model dependent)

## Performance

-   Google Tensor G5 processor
-   Improved on-device AI performance
-   Enhanced power efficiency
-   Faster app launches and multitasking

## Artificial Intelligence

-   Gemini integration across the system
-   AI-powered photo editing
-   Live Translate
-   Circle to Search
-   Recorder transcription and summaries
-   Smart Reply and writing assistance
-   AI call features (availability varies by region)

## Camera

-   Advanced computational photography
-   Night Sight
-   Super Res Zoom
-   Magic Editor
-   Magic Eraser
-   Best Take
-   Photo Unblur
-   Real Tone
-   4K video recording
-   Cinematic video features

## Battery

-   All-day battery life
-   Adaptive Battery
-   Fast wired charging
-   Wireless charging (supported models)
-   Battery Health optimization

## Security

-   Titan security architecture
-   Fingerprint authentication
-   Face Unlock
-   Theft protection features
-   Regular Android security updates
-   VPN support (availability may vary)

## Software

-   Android 16
-   Material You customization
-   Pixel-exclusive features
-   Seamless updates
-   Long-term OS and security update commitment

## Connectivity

-   5G
-   Wi-Fi 7 (supported models)
-   Bluetooth
-   NFC
-   USB-C
-   Dual SIM (model/carrier dependent)

## Productivity

-   Google Assistant / Gemini
-   Call Screen
-   Hold for Me
-   Direct My Call
-   Pixel Screenshots
-   Cross-device ecosystem integration

## Accessibility

-   Live Caption
-   Voice Access
-   Magnification
-   Hearing aid support
-   Accessibility Scanner

## Summary

Pixel 10 focuses on: - AI-first user experience - Improved camera
capabilities - Better battery efficiency - Strong security and privacy -
Long software support - Smooth Android experience
